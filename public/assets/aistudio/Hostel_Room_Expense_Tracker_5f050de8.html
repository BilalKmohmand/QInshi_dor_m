How it works

From a shared dinner to a zero balance

Anyone who pays for something shared adds one row. The sheet does the splitting, the running balances and the month-end settlement.

  1. Log the expenseDate, what it was, category, amount, who paid, and who shares it.
  2. Untick who was absentAll five are ticked by default. A dinner for three splits three ways.
  3. Log repaymentsWhen cash moves between roommates, record who gave and who received.
  4. Read the balancesPer month: paid versus share, plus the running outstanding balance.
  5. Settle the monthPick one coordinator, or exact person-to-person amounts. Log each transfer.

Ground rules

Five habits keep the numbers honest

Room rules

  • Log it the same day. The system only works if every shared purchase goes in.
  • Only shared purchases go in. Personal items do not.
  • Whoever pays, logs it. If someone asked you to bring something, log it when you get back.
  • Do not type in the grey columns (Split Between, Each Pays, Month) or in Balances, Settlement and Monthly Report. They calculate themselves.
  • A positive balance means the group owes you. A negative balance means you owe the group.

Before you start

  • Delete the sample rows in Expenses and Payments. They only show how the numbers flow.
  • Share the sheet with all five roommates as Editors, so anyone can log an expense from a phone.
  • To rename a roommate or change categories, edit the Settings tab. Every other tab updates.
  • Amounts show in yuan (¥). To change currency, use Format, Number, Custom currency on the amount columns.
